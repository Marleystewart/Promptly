Promptly

Privacy

Last updated 28 July 2026

Promptly tells students when an internship opens. To do that we need a little information about you. This page explains exactly what we keep, where it goes, and what we will never do with it — in plain English, not legal boilerplate.

Never leaves your device

Your résumé text, your profile photo, and your application progress (applied, interview, offer) are stored only in your own browser. They are never uploaded to our servers. If you clear your browser data, they are gone — we have no copy.

What we store on our servers

When you set up alerts, we save the details needed to match and reach you:

  • Your name and email address
  • School, graduation year, and major
  • Location preference, and whether you are open to remote or relocating
  • Interests and the alert fields you pick
  • Alerts you save and companies you choose to watch
  • Your notification preferences, and — if you turn on phone alerts — the push subscription your browser generates

That is the whole list. We do not ask for and do not want your government ID, Social Security number, bank details, or passwords for any other service.

Who processes it

We keep the number of companies touching your data as small as we can:

  • Vercel — hosts the app
  • Upstash — stores your alert profile
  • Resend — delivers your alert emails
  • Apple, Google, or Mozilla push services — deliver phone notifications, but only if you turn them on

Analytics

We count basic things — how many people opened the app today, how many alerts were clicked — so we know what is working. This is first-party only: the counts live in our own database, tied to a random session number, not to your name or email. There are no advertising cookies, no tracking pixels, and no ad networks anywhere in Promptly.

Company logos are served from our own servers. We deliberately do not use a third-party logo or favicon service, because that would tell another company which employers you are looking at.

We also record anonymous outcomes — that a student at a given school reached a given stage at a given company. This carries no name and no email, and it exists so we can eventually show students how their peers are doing.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to recruiters.
  • We never apply to a job on your behalf. Every alert links to the employer's own posting.
  • We never charge you to apply, and we will never ask you to pay an employer.
  • We never post anything anywhere as you.

Deleting everything

Open Profile → Settings → Delete My Data in the app. That removes your stored profile, your saved alerts, your watched companies, and your notification subscription. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a shadow copy. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Students under 18

Promptly is built for college students and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.
