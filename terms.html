Terms of use

Last updated 28 July 2026

Promptly is an alert service for students. We watch employers' own job systems and tell you when something opens. These are the ground rules — what we do, what we don't, and what we ask of you.

What Promptly is

Promptly monitors employers' public hiring systems and notifies you when a matching internship or early-career role appears. Every alert links to the employer's own official posting. Promptly is a signal, not an application service.

What Promptly is not

  • We are not an employer, a recruiter, or a staffing agency.
  • We do not submit applications for you, and never will.
  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with the companies whose postings we surface. We simply read their public job feeds.
  • We do not guarantee you an interview, an offer, or a job.

Free to use

Promptly is free for students. We will never charge you to apply to a job, and no legitimate employer will either. If any posting you find through us asks you to pay a fee, send money, or share bank or Social Security details before hiring you, that is a scam — do not proceed, and please report it to us so we can pull the source.

Accuracy, honestly stated

A posting appears in Promptly only while it is live in the employer's own feed, and drops out automatically once it is filled or pulled. That makes our listings unusually current — but we do not control employers' systems. Deadlines can change, roles can be cancelled, and a feed can go down. Always confirm the details on the employer's official posting before you rely on them. Promptly is provided as-is, with no warranty that the feed is complete or uninterrupted.

What we ask of you

  • Give us a real email address, so your alerts actually reach you.
  • Use Promptly for your own job search — do not scrape, resell, or redistribute the feed.
  • Do not try to break, overload, or abuse the service.
  • One account per person.

Your account and your data

You can delete everything at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete My Data. How we handle your information is covered in our Privacy page. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

Changes

Promptly is early and actively being built, so these terms will change as the product does. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top.

Contact

Questions, problems, or something that looks wrong: help.promptly@gmail.com. A real person reads it.
